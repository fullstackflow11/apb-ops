Privacy Policy

All Points Boats, Inc. · APB Operations · Last updated July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how All Points Boats, Inc. ("APB," "we," "us") handles information within the APB Operations application (the "App"), an internal business tool used by APB employees and authorized personnel.

Who this applies to

The App is an internal operations system. It is not a consumer product and is not offered to the general public. Access is limited to authorized APB staff and contractors.

Information we handle

The App processes business operations data, including: employee and technician names, timesheets and hours worked, job and project details, customer and vessel names, proposals, purchasing records, and related operational notes. This information is entered by APB personnel in the course of their work.

How we use it

Information is used solely to operate APB's business — scheduling and tracking work, managing projects and purchasing, reviewing and approving timesheets, and preparing invoices and payroll. With a user's authorization, timesheet and job data may be synchronized to third-party business systems such as Intuit QuickBooks Online for accounting and payroll purposes.

Third-party services

The App relies on trusted service providers to function, including Supabase (data storage) and, where connected, Intuit QuickBooks Online (accounting) and Resend (email notifications). Information shared with these providers is limited to what is required to perform their service and is governed by their respective privacy and security terms. We do not sell personal information.

Data security & retention

We take reasonable measures to protect the information in the App and retain it for as long as needed for legitimate business, accounting, and legal purposes. Access is restricted to authorized users through account-based sign-in.
