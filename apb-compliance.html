How This Works
  • Master Policies are APB's own coverage. When General Liability (1/1) or Workers Comp (6/1) renews, every certificate holder below needs a fresh COI issued — that's the trigger for the annual reissue cycle.
  • Certificate Holders are the marinas, yards, and clients APB has issued a Certificate of Insurance to. Each card shows the coverages on file (GL / WC / Auto) and their controlling expiration date.
  • Amber — Expiring ≤30d: A holder's COI lapses within 30 days. Request a renewal from Brown & Brown before it expires.
  • Red — Expired: The COI on file has lapsed. APB may not be cleared to work at that location until a current certificate is delivered.

Source of truth is the APB 2026 COIs library in SharePoint (linked above), mirrored in the Compliance channel in Teams. To add a new holder or update a date here, use Add Holder — then upload the actual certificate to the matching SharePoint folder. GL = General Liability · WC = Workers Comp · Auto = Commercial Auto.
