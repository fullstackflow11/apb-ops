What ISO 9001 is — and why it matters for APB

ISO 9001:2015 is the international standard for a Quality Management System (QMS). Certification is independent proof that APB has consistent, documented processes for delivering work that meets customer and regulatory requirements — and that it continually improves.

For a marine fabrication and repair shop it's a real competitive edge:

  • Opens doors: government, defense, and large commercial contracts increasingly require ISO 9001 as a prerequisite to bid.
  • Backs up class-society work: the traceability and document control ISO demands is exactly what ABS, Lloyd's, and RINA auditors look for.
  • Reduces rework & disputes: defined procedures mean fewer mistakes, and documented records protect APB if a job is ever questioned.
